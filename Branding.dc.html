In brief

InBold is a creative communications agency offering branding services from offices in Copenhagen (Denmark), Stockholm (Sweden), Oslo (Norway) and Saigon (Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam). Branding deliverables include brand strategy and positioning, naming, visual identity and design, messaging and tone of voice, brand guidelines and rebranding. Selected branding clients include Clipper Teas and Knitting for Olive. Contact: hello@inbold.com, +45 70 26 44 22.

What is branding?+

The strategy, identity and messaging that make a brand recognisable and consistent — far more than a logo.

How is branding different from a logo?+

A logo is one element; branding is the whole strategy, identity system and messaging behind it.

How long does a rebrand take?+

Typically a few months, depending on scope — from strategy to full rollout.

Which markets do you cover?+

Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon.
