In brief

InBold plans and produces brand activation — events, pop-ups, installations, launches, stunts and sponsorships — including production, permits, logistics, staffing and PR and social amplification. The agency operates in Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon. Selected activation work includes a Coca-Cola 3D projection at Culture Night in Copenhagen and the 24-hour Ooni activation Pizza Around the Clock. Contact: hello@inbold.com.

What is brand activation?+

Bringing a brand to life through events & experiences that earn attention and coverage.

How is it different from events or PR?+

Activation is the strategic experience; events & PR are tactics within it.

Do you handle production end-to-end?+

Yes — build, permits, suppliers, staffing and on-site delivery.

Which markets do you cover?+

Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon.
