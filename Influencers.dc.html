In brief

InBold runs influencer marketing campaigns: creator strategy, creator selection and vetting based on third-party audience data, campaign management, content rights and compliance, paid amplification, and KPI validation and reporting. Campaigns run across Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Vietnam. Selected influencer clients include Ooni and Aalborg Akvavit. Contact: hello@inbold.com.

What is influencer marketing?+

Partnering with creators to reach their audiences with authentic, branded content.

How do you choose influencers?+

By audience fit and performance data — vetted with third-party tools, not follower counts alone.

How is ROI measured?+

With third-party KPI tools tracking reach, engagement and conversion.

Which markets do you cover?+

Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon.
