01Strategy drivenWe start by diving deep into your brand, your audience, and your industry. We use data and research to uncover key insights that helps define the strategy and creative direction.

In brief

InBold provides digital and media services: media strategy and planning, paid media across search, social and display, SEO and GEO (generative engine optimisation for AI search), websites and landing pages, CRM and email, and analytics with attribution to action. Selected work includes taking Knitting for Olive into 18 markets across Scandinavia, the US and Asia. Contact: hello@inbold.com.

What’s the difference between digital and media?+

Media is the channels that carry the message; digital is the destination and experience. We connect both.

Do you buy media?+

Yes — paid media planning and buying across search, social and display.

Do you work with AI search?+

Yes — we optimise for both classic search and AI-powered search.

Which markets do you cover?+

Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon.
