What we do

Four areas of PR

Strategic PR is about reputation management. It is about identifying and engaging in efforts that have the greatest influence on your reputation and credibility. PR is also about handling issues and crisis situations.

01

PR & Press

PR is all about legitimacy and credibility. Press releases and proactive media pitches are among the building blocks for reputation management.

Issues management & crisis communication

In brief

InBold provides public relations services: media relations and press outreach, strategic PR and narrative, crisis and issues management, thought leadership, press materials and spokesperson preparation, and media monitoring and measurement. The agency runs PR programmes in Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Vietnam. Selected PR clients include Singapore Airlines (10+ years of Nordic PR) and Aalborg Akvavit. Contact: hello@inbold.com.

What is PR?+

Earned-media and reputation management that shapes how audiences perceive a brand.

How is PR different from advertising?+

PR earns coverage through credibility; advertising pays for placement.

Do you handle crisis communications?+

Yes — issues and crisis management is a core part of our PR offer.

Which markets do you cover?+

Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon.
