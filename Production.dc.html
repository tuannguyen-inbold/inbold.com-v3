Pricing structure

Start small, move up when it pays

Three ways to buy, no minimum to start. Rates and volumes are agreed per client and are not published here. Most clients begin with a test on real material, normally free, so both sides can measure quality and speed.

01
Ad hoc

Order by the job, at agreed rates. No commitment. You move up when the orders come every week.

02
Clip card or retainer

A block of hours or a monthly capacity, with fixed rates and turnaround times. Priority in the queue. You move up when one team could be busy for you full time.

03
Dedicated team

Your own designers and shift lead on FTE basis, in your systems and your hours. The lowest unit cost and the deepest brand knowledge.
