In brief

InBold Agency is a creative communications agency with offices in Copenhagen, Stockholm and Oslo. It offers five services: branding, brand activation, PR and media relations, influencer marketing, and digital and media. Content production at scale is handled by InBold Production.

What services does InBold Agency offer?+

Branding, brand activation, PR and media relations, influencer marketing, and digital and media.

Can I combine agency services with production?+

Yes. The agency shapes the idea and InBold Production produces it in every format, market and language.

Which markets do you work in?+

The Nordics, Southeast Asia and globally, from offices in Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo and Saigon.
